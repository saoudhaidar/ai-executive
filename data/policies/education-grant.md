# Adult Education Grant

## Eligibility
Residents aged 18 or older enrolled in an accredited local training or education program may apply.
Household income must be at or below $50,000 per year.

## Required documents
Applicants must provide proof of identity, proof of enrollment, proof of residency, and proof of household income.

## Fees and timelines
There is no application fee.
Applications are reviewed within 15 business days after all required documents are received.

## Application and review rules
Applicants must meet both the age and enrollment conditions as well as the household-income limit; satisfying one condition does not waive another.
Admission to a program without current enrollment does not satisfy the enrollment requirement.
The program must be both accredited and local, and proof of enrollment must correspond to the program for which the applicant is applying.
The 15-business-day review period starts after proof of identity, enrollment, residency, and household income have all been received.
This policy does not specify grant amounts, covered expenses, or renewal frequency; those details must not be inferred.
