# Home Repair Grant

## Eligibility
The applicant must own and occupy a home in the service area.
Household income must be at or below $55,000 per year, and the requested repair must address a health or safety issue.

## Required documents
Applicants must provide proof of ownership, proof of identity, proof of income, and a written repair estimate.

## Fees and timelines
There is no application fee.
An initial review takes up to 20 business days after all documents are received.

## Application and review rules
The initial review period begins only after proof of ownership, proof of identity, proof of income, and the written repair estimate have all been received.
The initial review is not a guarantee of grant approval or funding.
Cosmetic upgrades alone are not listed as a grant purpose.
The policy does not state whether work started before approval is reimbursable, so applicants should not assume those costs will be covered.
