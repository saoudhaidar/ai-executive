# Childcare Subsidy

## Eligibility
The applicant must live in the service area and care for a child under 13.
Household income must be at or below $60,000 per year, and the applicant must be working, studying, or seeking work.

## Required documents
Applicants must provide proof of identity, proof of income, the child's proof of age, and childcare provider details.

## Fees and timelines
There is no application fee.
Applicants receive a decision within 15 business days after submitting a complete application.

## Application and review rules
The 15-business-day decision period starts after proof of identity, proof of income, the child's proof of age, and childcare provider details have all been received.
Household income and the applicant's work, study, or job-search status are separate eligibility requirements; meeting one does not replace the other.
Provider details describe the proposed care arrangement but do not by themselves establish eligibility.
This policy does not state whether eligibility continues when a child turns 13 or specify a subsidy amount, so neither should be inferred.
