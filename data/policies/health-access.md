# Community Health Access

## Eligibility
Residents without active health coverage may request help finding a participating community clinic.
This service provides navigation support and does not determine medical eligibility or provide medical advice.

## Required documents
Applicants should provide proof of identity and proof of residency to help the navigator identify local options.
No medical records are required for an initial navigation appointment.

## Fees and timelines
Navigation support is free.
A navigator contacts applicants within 5 business days.

## Service boundaries
The five-business-day commitment is for navigator contact, not a guaranteed clinic appointment, enrollment decision, or start of medical care.
Navigation helps residents find participating community clinics but does not diagnose, treat, or determine medical eligibility.
No medical records are required for an initial navigation appointment; this does not establish what a clinic may request for later clinical care.
The policy does not specify clinic availability, appointment wait times, or coverage enrollment outcomes, so those results must not be promised.
