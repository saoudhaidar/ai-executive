# Community Food Assistance

## Eligibility
Households in the service area with income at or below $35,000 per year may receive a monthly food package.
Eligibility is reviewed every six months.

## Required documents
Applicants must provide proof of identity, proof of address, and proof of household income.

## Fees and timelines
Food packages are provided at no charge.
The team reviews complete applications within 3 business days.

## Application and review rules
The $35,000 annual income limit applies to the household, while the monthly food package is described as a household benefit.
A complete application includes proof of identity, proof of address, and proof of household income; the 3-business-day initial review period starts after all three are received.
The initial application review and the six-month eligibility review are separate steps; the six-month interval is not the initial decision timeline.
The policy does not specify package contents or delivery arrangements, so applicants should not be promised a particular item or delivery method.
