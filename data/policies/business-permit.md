# Small Business Permit

## Eligibility
Businesses operating within the service area must obtain a permit before opening to the public.
Applicants must identify the business location and business activity.

## Required documents
Applicants must provide a completed application, proof of business registration, and a site plan.

## Fees and timelines
The $75 application fee is non-refundable, including when an application is denied or withdrawn; permit review takes up to 12 business days of active review after a complete application is received.

## Review rules
A complete application means the completed application, proof of business registration, and site plan have all been received.
Submitting an application with any required item missing does not start the 12-business-day review clock.
If a reviewer requests clarification after review begins, the clock pauses while the requested information is outstanding and resumes when that information is received.
Time spent waiting for an applicant's clarification is not counted toward the 12 business days of active review.
The review period is not a guarantee of approval, and a business must obtain its permit before opening to the public.
An issued permit applies only to the approved location and business activity; a material change to either requires an amended application and approval before operating under the change.
