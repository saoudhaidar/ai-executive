# Rental Assistance

## Eligibility
Applicants must live in the service area and have household income at or below $50,000 per year.
The program is available to renters with a current lease or a notice to vacate.

## Required documents
Applicants must provide proof of identity, proof of household income, and either a current lease or a notice to vacate; both housing documents are not required.
A complete application also includes a signed consent form for verification.

## Fees and timelines
There is no application fee.
The service team reviews complete applications within 10 business days.

## Application and review rules
The 10-business-day review period starts after all required documents and the signed consent form have been received.
Consent allows verification but is not an approval or a guarantee of payment.
The policy does not specify assistance amounts, covered months, or payment dates, so those details must not be inferred.
