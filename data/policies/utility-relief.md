# Utility Relief

## Eligibility
Applicants must be residential utility account holders in the service area.
Household income must be at or below $45,000 per year.

## Required documents
Applicants must provide a recent utility bill, proof of identity, and proof of household income.

## Fees and timelines
There is no application fee.
A complete application is reviewed within 7 business days.

## Application and review rules
A complete application includes a recent utility bill, proof of identity, and proof of household income; the seven-business-day review period starts after all three are received.
Residential account-holder status and the household-income limit are separate eligibility requirements.
The utility bill supports the account-holder requirement but does not by itself establish income eligibility.
The policy does not specify a relief amount or whether applying prevents a disconnection, so neither outcome should be promised.
