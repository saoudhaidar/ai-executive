# Senior Support Services

## Eligibility
Residents aged 65 or older may request a needs assessment for in-home support.
The service area residency requirement applies.

## Required documents
Applicants must provide proof of identity, proof of age, and a completed needs-assessment form.

## Fees and timelines
The initial needs assessment is free.
The team contacts applicants to schedule an assessment within 10 business days.

## Assessment process
The 10-business-day commitment is to contact the applicant to schedule; it is not a promise that the assessment itself will be completed within that period.
The completed needs-assessment form supports the request for an assessment but does not itself guarantee that in-home support will be approved or available.
The initial assessment is free; this policy does not state whether any ongoing support has a cost.
The policy does not specify assessment duration or service availability, so neither should be promised.
