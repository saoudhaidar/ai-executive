# Public Transit Discount

## Eligibility
Residents aged 65 or older or residents with a qualifying disability may apply for a reduced-fare transit card.
The discount applies to local public transit fares only.

## Required documents
Applicants must provide proof of identity and proof of age or qualifying disability.

## Fees and timelines
There is no application fee.
Eligible applicants receive a transit card within 5 business days after approval.

## Application and use rules
Age 65 or older and qualifying disability are alternative eligibility paths; applicants provide proof of age or proof of qualifying disability, as applicable.
The five-business-day card timeline begins after approval, not when an application is submitted.
The reduced fare applies only to local public transit fares and does not establish a discount for other transportation services.
The policy does not define qualifying disabilities or state a specific fare amount, so neither should be inferred.
